Pathology Report

Specimen: Right breast, needle core biopsy. Clinical history: 52-year-old female with a 1.8 cm spiculated mass in the right upper outer quadrant identified on screening mammography, classified as BI-RADS 5. Gross description: Received in formalin are four cores of tan-white tissue, each measuring 1.2 cm in length and 0.1 cm in diameter. The entire specimen is submitted in one cassette. Microscopic description: Sections reveal infiltrating ductal carcinoma, moderately differentiated, Nottingham grade 2 of 3. Tumor cells are arranged in cords, nests, and tubular structures within a desmoplastic stroma. Nuclear grade is intermediate with occasional mitotic figures. No lymphovascular invasion is identified. Margins cannot be assessed on core biopsy material.
