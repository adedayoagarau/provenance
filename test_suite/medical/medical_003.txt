Surgical Procedure Note

Procedure: Laparoscopic cholecystectomy. Anesthesia: General endotracheal. The patient was placed in supine position with arms tucked. After induction of general anesthesia, a Veress needle was inserted at the umbilicus and pneumoperitoneum was established to 15 mmHg. Four trocars were placed in standard positions. The gallbladder was identified and the cystic duct and cystic artery were dissected within the triangle of Calot. Critical view of safety was achieved and documented photographically. The cystic duct and cystic artery were clipped with titanium clips and divided. The gallbladder was dissected from the liver bed using electrocautery and removed through the umbilical port in a specimen bag. Hemostasis was confirmed and all ports were removed under direct visualization.
