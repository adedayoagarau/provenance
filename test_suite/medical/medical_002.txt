Drug Information Leaflet

Metformin hydrochloride is an oral antihyperglycemic agent used in the management of type 2 diabetes mellitus. The recommended starting dose is 500mg twice daily or 850mg once daily, administered with meals to reduce gastrointestinal side effects. The maximum recommended daily dose is 2550mg. Common adverse effects include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, flatulence, and abdominal discomfort, which typically resolve with continued use. The most serious adverse effect is lactic acidosis, which is rare but potentially fatal. Metformin is contraindicated in patients with severe renal impairment, acute or chronic metabolic acidosis, and in patients undergoing radiologic studies involving intravascular administration of iodinated contrast materials.
