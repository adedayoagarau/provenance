Public Health Advisory

The Department of Health is issuing this advisory regarding an increase in reported cases of respiratory syncytial virus in the metropolitan area. Over the past four weeks, emergency department visits for RSV-related illness have increased by 340 percent compared to the same period last year. The majority of hospitalizations have occurred in children under 24 months and adults over 65 years of age. Healthcare providers are advised to implement enhanced infection control measures and consider prophylactic treatment with palivizumab for high-risk infants. The public is encouraged to practice frequent handwashing, avoid close contact with sick individuals, and seek medical attention for symptoms including persistent cough, wheezing, and difficulty breathing.
