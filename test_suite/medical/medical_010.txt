Mental Health Assessment

The patient is a 28-year-old female presenting for initial psychiatric evaluation with chief complaint of persistent low mood and difficulty concentrating for the past three months. She reports decreased appetite with unintentional weight loss of approximately 8 pounds, insomnia with difficulty initiating sleep, low energy, feelings of worthlessness, and diminished interest in previously enjoyed activities. She denies suicidal ideation, homicidal ideation, auditory or visual hallucinations, and substance use. PHQ-9 score is 18, indicating moderately severe depression. GAD-7 score is 12, indicating moderate anxiety. Mental status examination reveals a cooperative young woman with congruent affect, psychomotor retardation, and goal-directed thought process without looseness of associations or tangentiality.
