Non-Disclosure Agreement

The Receiving Party agrees that for a period of five years from the date of disclosure, it shall hold and maintain the Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in strict confidence for the sole and exclusive benefit of the Disclosing Party. The Receiving Party shall carefully restrict access to Confidential Information to employees, contractors, and third parties as is reasonably required and shall require those persons to sign nondisclosure restrictions at least as protective as those in this Agreement. The Receiving Party shall not, without the prior written approval of the Disclosing Party, use for the Receiving Partys own benefit, publish, copy, or otherwise disclose to others, or permit the use by others for their benefit or to the detriment of the Disclosing Party.
