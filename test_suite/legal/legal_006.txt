Employment Agreement Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing thirty days written notice to the other party. Upon termination, the Employee shall return all company property, documents, and materials, including electronic copies thereof. The Company shall pay to the Employee all accrued but unpaid salary, earned but unused vacation time, and any approved but unreimbursed expenses through the date of termination. Sections relating to confidentiality, non-competition, non-solicitation, and intellectual property shall survive termination of this Agreement and remain in full force and effect.
