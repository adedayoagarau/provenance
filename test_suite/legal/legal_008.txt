Arbitration Clause

Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, or the breach, termination, or invalidity thereof, shall be settled by arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association in accordance with its Commercial Arbitration Rules. The arbitration shall be conducted by a single arbitrator selected in accordance with the aforementioned rules. The place of arbitration shall be New York, New York. The language of the arbitration shall be English. The arbitral award shall be final and binding upon the parties. Judgment upon the award rendered by the arbitrator may be entered in any court having jurisdiction thereof.
