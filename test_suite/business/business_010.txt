Partnership Proposal

We propose a strategic partnership between our organizations to jointly develop and market an integrated solution for the healthcare vertical. The proposed structure involves a revenue-sharing model with a 60/40 split favoring the primary seller on each transaction. Joint development costs would be shared equally, with an estimated combined investment of 3.5 million dollars over 18 months. The addressable market for the integrated solution is estimated at 2.1 billion dollars annually, with a realistic first-year target of capturing 0.5 percent market share. Key advantages of the partnership include complementary technology capabilities, non-overlapping customer bases, and combined domain expertise.
