Project Status Update

Project Atlas is currently in Phase 3 of 5 and tracking two weeks behind the original timeline due to unexpected complexity in the data migration workstream. The delay is attributed to legacy system data quality issues that required development of additional cleansing and transformation logic. The technical team has proposed a revised approach that should recover one week of the delay. Key milestones completed this month include successful integration testing of the payment processing module, completion of user acceptance testing for the dashboard, and deployment of the API gateway to the staging environment. Three critical path items remain for Phase 3 completion.
