Marketing Strategy Memo

Following analysis of our Q2 campaign performance data, we recommend a significant reallocation of the digital marketing budget for the upcoming quarter. Paid social channels delivered a customer acquisition cost of 47 dollars, compared to 28 dollars for search engine marketing and 19 dollars for email campaigns targeting existing customers. We propose reducing paid social spend by 35 percent and redirecting those funds toward search and email automation. Additionally, our A/B testing program identified that video content generates 3.2 times higher engagement than static images across all platforms. We recommend increasing video production capacity accordingly.
