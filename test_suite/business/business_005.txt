Vendor Evaluation Report

After evaluating six enterprise CRM platforms against our defined requirements matrix, we recommend proceeding with Vendor C for the following reasons. Vendor C scored highest in overall functionality with 87 out of 100 points, compared to 82 for Vendor A and 79 for Vendor B. Total cost of ownership over five years is estimated at 2.3 million dollars, which is 15 percent lower than the next closest option. Implementation timeline is projected at 14 weeks, the shortest among all evaluated vendors. Customer references were uniformly positive, with particular praise for the vendors implementation methodology and ongoing support quality.
