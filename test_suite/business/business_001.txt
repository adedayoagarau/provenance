Quarterly Earnings Summary

Total revenue for Q3 2024 reached 4.7 billion dollars, representing a 12 percent increase year-over-year and exceeding analyst consensus estimates by approximately 200 million dollars. Gross margins expanded to 68.3 percent, up from 65.1 percent in the prior year quarter, driven primarily by improved operational efficiency and favorable product mix. Operating expenses as a percentage of revenue declined to 41.2 percent from 43.8 percent. Free cash flow generation was robust at 1.1 billion dollars, enabling the company to reduce net debt by 800 million dollars while returning 300 million dollars to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases.
