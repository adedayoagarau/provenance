Board Meeting Minutes

The Board of Directors convened at 10:00 AM on March 15, 2024. All seven directors were present. The minutes of the previous meeting held on December 12, 2023 were approved unanimously. The Chief Financial Officer presented the annual financial results, noting record revenue and margin expansion. The board approved the proposed annual dividend of 2.40 dollars per share, payable on April 30 to shareholders of record as of April 15. The Compensation Committee presented its recommendations for executive compensation adjustments. After discussion, the board approved a 7 percent base salary increase for the CEO and performance-based equity grants for the senior leadership team.
