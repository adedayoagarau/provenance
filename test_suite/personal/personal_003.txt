Product Review

I bought this blender about three months ago and honestly its been great. Handles frozen fruit no problem, makes smoothies in like 30 seconds, and its way quieter than my old one. The only downside is the lid doesnt seal super well if you fill it past the max line, which I learned the hard way when I got mango smoothie all over my ceiling. Wouldnt recommend that. But overall for the price its really solid. Ive used it pretty much every day and it still works perfectly.
