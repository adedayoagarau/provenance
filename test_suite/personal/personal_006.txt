Moving Day Story

The move was an absolute disaster. The truck showed up two hours late, then it started raining, then we realized the couch wouldnt fit through the apartment door. My buddy Carlos had the genius idea to try the window which actually worked but took about 45 minutes and a lot of creative problem solving. By 8pm we had everything inside but nothing unpacked and we were all completely wiped. Ordered pizza and sat on the floor eating it because the table was buried under boxes somewhere. But hey, new apartment. New chapter.
