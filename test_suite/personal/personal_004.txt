Okay so this banana bread recipe is the one Ive been making for years and it never fails. You need 3 really ripe bananas like the grosser the better, a third cup of melted butter, three quarters cup of sugar, one egg, a teaspoon of vanilla, a teaspoon of baking soda, a pinch of salt, and one and a half cups of flour. Mash the bananas, mix in the butter, then add everything else. Pour into a greased loaf pan and bake at 350 for about an hour. The trick is to not overmix the batter. Lumpy is fine. Trust me.
