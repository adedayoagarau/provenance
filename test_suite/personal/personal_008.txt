Vacation Recap

Just got back from a week in Costa Rica and wow. The highlight was definitely the cloud forest in Monteverde. You literally walk through clouds, its surreal. We saw like four different species of hummingbirds and a sloth just hanging out in a tree right next to the path. The zip lining was terrifying but amazing. Food was incredible everywhere we went. Only complaint is the roads are pretty rough outside the main cities. Our rental car took a beating. But honestly it was the best trip weve taken in years.
