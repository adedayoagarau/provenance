Legislative Analysis

Senate Bill 1247 would amend the existing telecommunications regulatory framework to impose net neutrality requirements on internet service providers. The bill prohibits blocking, throttling, and paid prioritization of lawful internet traffic. It grants enforcement authority to the Federal Communications Commission with the power to levy fines of up to 2 million dollars per violation. Industry groups estimate compliance costs at approximately 1.4 billion dollars annually across the sector. Consumer advocacy organizations argue that the bill does not go far enough, noting the absence of provisions addressing data caps and zero-rating practices. The Congressional Budget Office estimates the bill would generate 340 million dollars in fine revenue over ten years.
