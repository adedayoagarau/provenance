Policy Proposal - Infrastructure

The proposed Infrastructure Renewal Act would authorize 420 billion dollars in federal spending over ten years to rebuild the nations deteriorating transportation network. The legislation allocates 180 billion dollars for highway and bridge repair, targeting the estimated 46,154 structurally deficient bridges currently in service. An additional 120 billion dollars would fund expansion of public transit systems in metropolitan areas with populations exceeding 500,000. The remaining 120 billion dollars would support rural broadband deployment, water system modernization, and electric vehicle charging infrastructure. Funding mechanisms include a combination of increased fuel taxes, public-private partnerships, and reallocation of existing transportation appropriations.
