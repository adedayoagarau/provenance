Electoral Commission Report

The Electoral Commission certifies the results of the general election held on November 5, 2024. Total registered voters numbered 168,342,876. Total ballots cast were 131,276,493, representing a turnout rate of 78.0 percent, the highest since 1960. Of ballots cast, 129,891,007 were certified as valid, with 1,385,486 rejected due to irregularities including incomplete markings, duplicate submissions, and late receipt of mail-in ballots. Provisional ballots totaled 2,847,291, of which 2,103,445 were ultimately accepted after verification. The commission received 347 formal complaints regarding polling place operations, of which 12 were substantiated and referred for further investigation.
