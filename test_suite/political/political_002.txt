Campaign Speech Excerpt

When I talk to families in this district, I hear the same concerns over and over again. They worry about whether they can afford to send their kids to college. They worry about what happens if someone in their family gets sick. They worry about whether there will be good jobs for their children when they grow up. These are not partisan concerns. These are human concerns. And they deserve serious answers, not talking points. I am running for this office because I believe we can do better. Not by tearing each other down, but by building something together that works for everyone in this community.
