UN Resolution Summary

The General Assembly adopted Resolution A/RES/78/142 by a vote of 143 in favor, 9 against, with 25 abstentions. The resolution calls upon all Member States to accelerate the implementation of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, with particular emphasis on climate action, poverty eradication, and gender equality. The resolution further urges developed countries to fulfill their commitment of mobilizing 100 billion dollars annually in climate finance for developing nations. It requests the Secretary-General to submit a comprehensive progress report to the General Assembly at its seventy-ninth session, including recommendations for addressing implementation gaps.
