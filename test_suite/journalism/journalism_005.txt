Sports: Championship Recap

In what many are calling the greatest championship game in the tournaments 47-year history, the underdogs from Central State completed a stunning comeback to defeat top-seeded Northern University 87-84 in triple overtime. Down by 17 points with six minutes remaining in regulation, Central State launched an improbable rally fueled by sophomore guard Marcus Williams, who scored 14 of his career-high 38 points in the final stretch. The victory marks Central States first national championship and caps a remarkable season in which the team was picked to finish last in its conference.
