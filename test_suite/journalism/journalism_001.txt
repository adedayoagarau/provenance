Breaking: Market Rally Continues

Wall Street extended its winning streak on Thursday as investors digested better-than-expected earnings reports from major technology companies. The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 285 points, or 0.7 percent, while the broader market gained ground across nearly every sector. Trading volume was heavy, with more than 4.2 billion shares changing hands on the New York Stock Exchange. Analysts pointed to strong corporate profit margins and resilient consumer spending as key drivers of the rally. However, some strategists cautioned that valuations have become stretched and that a pullback could be imminent.
