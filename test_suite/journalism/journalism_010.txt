Profile: Tech Founder Interview

Sarah Chen does not look like someone who just closed a 400 million dollar funding round. Sitting in the modest conference room of her companys Oakland headquarters, wearing jeans and a faded university sweatshirt, she speaks quietly about the platform she has spent seven years building. The company, which uses machine learning to optimize supply chains for mid-sized manufacturers, now serves more than 2,300 clients across 14 countries. Revenue has grown by 340 percent over the past two years. But Chen insists the company is still in its early days.
