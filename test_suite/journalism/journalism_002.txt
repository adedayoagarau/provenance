Feature: Small Town Revival

When the last factory in Millbrook shuttered its doors in 2019, most people assumed the town would simply fade away. Five years later, the picture looks remarkably different. A combination of remote work migration, state tax incentives, and a grassroots revitalization effort has transformed this once-struggling community into one of the fastest-growing small towns in the region. Population has grown by 23 percent since 2020, new businesses are opening at a rate not seen since the 1970s, and the old factory building itself has been converted into a mixed-use development housing a brewery, coworking space, and artist studios.
