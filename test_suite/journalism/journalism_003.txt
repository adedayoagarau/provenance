Investigation: Water Quality Crisis

An investigation spanning six months and involving analysis of more than 2,000 water quality reports has revealed that dozens of municipalities across the state have been systematically underreporting contaminant levels in their public water systems. Documents obtained through freedom of information requests show that testing protocols were modified in ways that consistently produced lower readings. State environmental officials, speaking on condition of anonymity, confirmed that the practice was widespread and had been flagged internally as early as 2018 without corrective action being taken.
