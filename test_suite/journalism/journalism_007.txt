Health: New Treatment Breakthrough

Researchers at three major medical centers have published results from a Phase III clinical trial showing that a new class of immunotherapy drugs can achieve complete remission in patients with previously untreatable forms of pancreatic cancer. The study, published in the New England Journal of Medicine, followed 847 patients over four years. Those receiving the experimental treatment showed a 67 percent reduction in tumor size compared to the control group. While the results are preliminary and the treatment carries significant side effects, oncologists are calling it the most promising development in pancreatic cancer research in decades.
