Culture: Music Festival Review

Three days, four stages, 73 acts, and roughly 45,000 attendees descended upon Riverside Park for what has become the regions defining cultural event. The festival, now in its twelfth year, has evolved from a scrappy indie gathering into a professionally produced celebration of musical diversity. Highlights included a transcendent headlining set from veteran jazz pianist Ahmad Delacroix, a surprise acoustic performance from pop star Elena Chen in the intimate tent stage, and an incendiary hip-hop showcase that kept the crowd moving well past midnight.
