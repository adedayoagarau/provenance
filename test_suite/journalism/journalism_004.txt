Opinion: Education Reform Needed

The current education system was designed for a world that no longer exists. We continue to sort students by age rather than ability, measure success through standardized tests that reward memorization over creativity, and prepare young people for jobs that are rapidly disappearing. The evidence is overwhelming that this model is failing. Graduation rates tell only part of the story. The more troubling statistic is that fewer than half of college graduates report that their education was relevant to their current work. We need a fundamental reimagining of what school is for.
